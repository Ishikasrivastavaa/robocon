EL-01 / LOGIC LEVEL

The chat raised a possible 5 V sensor output versus 3.3 V ESP32-S3 GPIO issue. Check the exact board/output circuit before direct connection; add level shifting if required.

VERIFY ELECTRICALLY
FW-02 / PWM MAPPING

SERVO GPIO

GPIO 36 was proposed in discussion and then questioned. Do not treat it as a final pin mapping; verify the exact board pinout and PWM-capable assignment before soldering.

PIN MAP NOT FINAL
PWR-03 / MOTOR DRIVER

CURRENT + TOPOLOGY

Driver candidates were discussed, but the final model is not confirmed. Match driver topology and continuous/stall current to the selected motors; do not assume an IC/module can drive both motors without checking its datasheet.

MODEL / RATINGS TBD
PWR-04 / DISTRIBUTION

BATTERY, BUCKS & SWITCH

Two buck converters and a main switch were discussed. Confirm battery configuration, regulator output/current, servo supply, common ground and power-switch rating from the actual schematic.

REVIEW SCHEMATIC
ME-05 / STEERING

SERVO + LINKAGE

One servo is intended to drive the front Ackermann linkage. Verify steering travel, horn geometry, linkage clearance and servo load with the final chassis.

BENCH / MECHANICAL TEST
FB-06 / ENCODERS

RPM + ODOMETRY

Encoders are intended to support wheel RPM feedback and odometry. Confirm wiring, pulse counting and direction behavior before using their values in control.
